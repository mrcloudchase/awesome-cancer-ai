# Archive

Projects that mattered but are no longer maintained, have been archived by their authors, or carry licenses that make them hard to use today. Kept here for reference and citation. See the [main list](README.md) for active projects.

## Pathology

- [HistoSSLscaling](https://github.com/owkin/HistoSSLscaling) - Phikon self-supervised pathology encoders trained on TCGA; custom non-commercial license, last updated January 2024.

## Radiology

- [OncoNet](https://github.com/yala/OncoNet_Public) - Early mammography risk, triage and density models from the Barzilay lab, superseded by Mirai.
- [FeTS Front-End](https://github.com/FETS-AI/Front-End) - Federated glioma segmentation platform behind the first large federated learning study in cancer; custom license, no commits since 2023.
- [MedicalNet](https://github.com/Tencent/MedicalNet) - Pretrained 3D ResNets for medical transfer learning, dormant since 2019.

## Genomics

- [AlphaMissense](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphamissense) - Proteome-wide missense pathogenicity predictions under CC BY 4.0; repository archived May 2025.

## Drug Discovery

- [ESM (Meta)](https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm) - ESM-2 and ESMFold protein language models; repository archived August 2024 in favor of EvolutionaryScale's ESM.
- [DrugCell](https://github.com/idekerlab/DrugCell) - Interpretable neural network predicting cancer cell line drug response from GDSC and CTRP data, unmaintained since 2020.
- [DeepCDR](https://github.com/kimmo1019/DeepCDR) - Graph convolutional cancer drug response model on CCLE and GDSC, unmaintained since 2023.
